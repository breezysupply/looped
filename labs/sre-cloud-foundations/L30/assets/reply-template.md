# Reply to the customer (L30)

Write this for the customer's integration engineer: plain language, no internal jargon, no
blame, nothing you cannot support with your reproduction. Quote at least one request id from
YOUR reproduction against the fixture. Never include a key, an Authorization header or the
customer's document text.

## Summary

(two or three sentences: what is happening, whose side the fix is on, how confident you are)

## What we saw

(the request id(s), status, headers or stream events that support the summary)

## What it means

(caller, authentication/entitlement or service problem — and why you can tell)

## Next steps

(what the customer should do now, and what you will do; when they will hear from you next)

## Retry guidance

(when retrying helps, when it makes things worse, and the limits you recommend)
